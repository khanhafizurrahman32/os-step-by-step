    3-week plan¶

    About 1–1.5 hours a day, 6 days a week. Day 7 of each week is a light review day. Reviews (R1, R2…) follow the recall system and take 5–10 minutes each.

    Tick the boxes as you go — on GitHub you can fork the repo and edit this page to track progress.

    Week 1 — Virtualization¶

    Day New lesson Reviews
    1 ☐ L01 Intro + L02 Processes —
    2 ☐ L03 Limited direct execution R1: L01–02
    3 ☐ L04 Scheduling basics R1: L03
    4 ☐ L05 MLFQ R1: L04 · R2: L01–02
    5 ☐ L06 Address spaces + L07 Segmentation R1: L05 · R2: L03
    6 ☐ L08 Free space + L09 Paging R1: L06–07 · R2: L04
    7 Light day — redraw the CPU chain and memory chain from the chain map R2: L05

    Week 2 — Finish memory, then concurrency¶

    Day New lesson Reviews
    8 ☐ L10 TLBs R1: L08–09 · R3: L01–03
    9 ☐ L11 Smaller tables R1: L10 · R2: L06–07
    10 ☐ L12 Swapping R1: L11 · R2: L08–09 · R3: L04–05
    11 ☐ L13 Threads + L14 Locks R1: L12 · R2: L10
    12 ☐ L15 Concurrent DS + L16 Condition variables R1: L13–14 · R2: L11 · R3: L06–07
    13 ☐ L17 Semaphores + L18 Deadlock R1: L15–16 · R2: L12
    14 Light day — mixed drills, Virtualization + Concurrency R2: L13–14

    Week 3 — Persistence and distribution¶

    Day New lesson Reviews
    15 ☐ L19 I/O + L20 Disks R1: L17–18 · R2: L15–16 · R3: L08–10
    16 ☐ L21 RAID R1: L19–20 · R2: L17–18
    17 ☐ L22 Files API + L23 VSFS R1: L21 · R3: L11–12
    18 ☐ L24 FFS + L25 Journaling R1: L22–23 · R2: L19–20 · R3: L13–14
    19 ☐ L26 LFS & integrity + L27 NFS/AFS R1: L24–25 · R2: L21 · R3: L15–18
    20 Full mixed drill + cheat sheet from memory R2: L22–25
    21 Final: blurt all 5 chains, then fix gaps R3: L19–27

    Then keep doing R4 (Day +21) reviews with Anki — 10 minutes a day keeps it all.

    Short on time? The 7-day crash version¶

    Only the lessons that carry the most marks:

    1. L02 + L03 (processes, LDE)
    2. L04 + L05 (scheduling, MLFQ)
    3. L09 + L10 + L11 (paging, TLB, multi-level)
    4. L12 (swapping, LRU, clock)
    5. L13 + L14 + L16 (races, locks, condition variables)
    6. L17 + L18 (semaphores, deadlock)
    7. L23 + L25 (inodes, journaling)
